Foreman — a job queue in Postgres

A tenant with a huge pile of jobs can't make the other tenants wait.

How it works

Workers

1Tenant A has 240,000 jobs waiting. Workers are busy with them.

2Tenant B adds one job.

3Foreman gives workers jobs from both tenants in turn.

4B's job runs now, not after A's 240,000.
